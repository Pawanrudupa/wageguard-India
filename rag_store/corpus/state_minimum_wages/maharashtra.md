# Statutory Minimum Wages — State of Maharashtra
Notification Reference: Notification CL/MWA/15/2023/MW/Zone-I. Effective from 01 January 2024 to 30 June 2024 (Revisable bi-annually with Special Allowance / VDA).

## Overview and Zonal Classification
Minimum rates of wages in Maharashtra are fixed under Section 3 & 4 of the Minimum Wages Act, 1948, categorized into Zone I (Municipal Corporations of Greater Mumbai, Pune, Thane, Navi Mumbai, Nagpur, Nashik), Zone II (other municipal corporations and 'A' class municipalities), and Zone III (remaining rural and semi-urban areas). Special Allowance (VDA) is declared twice each year (in January and July).

## Scheduled Rates by Sector in Zone I (Unskilled Category, Effective Jan 2024)
- **Construction (Building Operations and Construction)**:
  - Basic: ₹440.00/day + VDA: ₹92.00/day = Total Daily: ₹532.00
  - Total Monthly: ₹13,832.00 per month
- **Retail & Commercial (Shops and Commercial Establishments)**:
  - Basic: ₹425.00/day + VDA: ₹92.00/day = Total Daily: ₹517.00
  - Total Monthly: ₹13,442.00 per month
- **Manufacturing & Factories (Textiles, Plastics, Engineering)**:
  - Basic: ₹430.00/day + VDA: ₹92.00/day = Total Daily: ₹522.00
  - Total Monthly: ₹13,572.00 per month
- **Security & Facility (Security Guards and Watchmen)**:
  - Basic: ₹460.00/day + VDA: ₹92.00/day = Total Daily: ₹552.00
  - Total Monthly: ₹14,352.00 per month (Covered under Maharashtra Private Security Guards Board regulations)
- **Hospitality & Food Services (Hotels, Restaurants, Eating Houses)**:
  - Basic: ₹415.00/day + VDA: ₹92.00/day = Total Daily: ₹507.00
  - Total Monthly: ₹13,182.00 per month
- **Domestic Work (Cleaners, Cooks, Domestic Helpers)**:
  - Basic: ₹390.00/day + VDA: ₹92.00/day = Total Daily: ₹482.00
  - Total Monthly: ₹12,532.00 per month
- **Agriculture & Allied (Agricultural Labour)**:
  - Basic: ₹350.00/day + VDA: ₹92.00/day = Total Daily: ₹442.00
  - Total Monthly: ₹11,492.00 per month
- **Transport & Logistics (Public Motor Transport and Goods Handling)**:
  - Basic: ₹435.00/day + VDA: ₹92.00/day = Total Daily: ₹527.00
  - Total Monthly: ₹13,702.00 per month

## Skill Differentials in Maharashtra
- Semi-skilled: Approximately 10% to 15% above the unskilled basic wage rate.
- Skilled: Approximately 25% to 30% above the unskilled basic wage rate.
- Highly Skilled: Approximately 40% above the unskilled basic wage rate.

## Enforcement and Legal Rules
1. Wage Period & Payment Date: Under Section 5 of Payment of Wages Act, employers with under 1,000 employees must pay wages before the 7th day of the subsequent month.
2. Overtime: Double the ordinary rate of wages (2x) for work done beyond 9 hours in a day or 48 hours in a week (Maharashtra Minimum Wages Rules, 1963, Rule 25).
3. Grievances: Office of the Labour Commissioner, Kamgar Bhavan, BKC, Bandra (E), Mumbai 400051.
