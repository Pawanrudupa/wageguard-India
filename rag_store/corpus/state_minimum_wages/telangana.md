# Statutory Minimum Wages — State of Telangana
Notification Reference: G.O.Ms.No. 43 Labour Employment Training & Factories Department. Effective 01 April 2024 to 30 September 2024 (Bi-annual VDA adjustment).

## Overview and Zonal Structure
Telangana fixes minimum rates of wages comprising a Basic Wage and a Cost of Living Allowance (CPI-linked VDA). Zone I covers the Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation (GHMC), Warangal, Nizamabad, and other municipal corporations. Zone II covers other municipalities and rural areas. For the period from 01 April 2024, the notified VDA is ₹326.00 per day.

## Scheduled Rates by Sector in Zone I (GHMC / Hyderabad Area, Unskilled Category)
- **Construction (Building Operations and Construction of Roads)**:

## Compliance and Overtime Rules
1. Telangana Shops and Establishments Act, 1988: Overtime is strictly payable at double (2x) the regular wage rate.
2. Payment of Wages Act Section 5(2): Wages upon termination/resignation must be paid before the expiry of the second working day.
3. Grievances Authority: Joint Commissioner of Labour, T. Anjaiah Bhavan, RTC Cross Roads, Musheerabad, Hyderabad 500020.
