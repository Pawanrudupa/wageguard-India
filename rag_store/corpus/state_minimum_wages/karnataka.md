# Statutory Minimum Wages — State of Karnataka
Notification Reference: Notification KLR/MW/Notif/2024-25/Zone-1. Effective 01 April 2024 to 31 March 2025.

## Overview and Zonal Structure
Karnataka classifies wages by Zones: Zone I covers Bruhat Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike (BBMP) and Agglomeration Areas; Zone II covers other City Corporations; Zone III covers District Headquarters; Zone IV covers other areas. Variable Dearness Allowance (VDA) is revised annually on 1st April based on the State Consumer Price Index (CPI points). For 2024-25, VDA is ₹125.00 per day (₹3,250.00 per month).

## Scheduled Rates by Sector in Zone I (BBMP / Bengaluru Area, Unskilled Category)
- **Construction (Building Construction, Maintenance & Road Operations)**:
  - Basic: ₹485.00/day + VDA: ₹125.00/day = Total Daily: ₹610.00
  - Total Monthly: ₹15,860.00 per month
- **Retail & Commercial (Shops and Commercial Establishments)**:
  - Basic: ₹470.00/day + VDA: ₹125.00/day = Total Daily: ₹595.00
  - Total Monthly: ₹15,470.00 per month
- **Manufacturing & Factories (Garment Industry, General Engineering, Electronics)**:
  - Basic: ₹465.00/day + VDA: ₹125.00/day = Total Daily: ₹590.00
  - Total Monthly: ₹15,340.00 per month
- **Security & Facility (Private Security Agencies and Watch and Ward)**:
  - Basic: ₹510.00/day + VDA: ₹125.00/day = Total Daily: ₹635.00
  - Total Monthly: ₹16,510.00 per month
- **Hospitality & Food Services (Hotels, Eateries, Bakeries, Canteens)**:
  - Basic: ₹450.00/day + VDA: ₹125.00/day = Total Daily: ₹575.00
  - Total Monthly: ₹14,950.00 per month
- **Domestic Work (Domestic Workers and Cleaners)**:
  - Basic: ₹430.00/day + VDA: ₹125.00/day = Total Daily: ₹555.00
  - Total Monthly: ₹14,430.00 per month
- **Agriculture & Allied (Agricultural Operations and Horticulture)**:
  - Basic: ₹390.00/day + VDA: ₹125.00/day = Total Daily: ₹515.00
  - Total Monthly: ₹13,390.00 per month
- **Transport & Logistics (Public Motor Transport and Cargo Handling)**:
  - Basic: ₹475.00/day + VDA: ₹125.00/day = Total Daily: ₹600.00
  - Total Monthly: ₹15,600.00 per month

## Skill Levels and Multipliers
- Semi-skilled workers: approx 10% higher than unskilled.
- Skilled workers (electricians, masons, mechanics, technicians): approx 20%-25% higher than unskilled.
- Highly skilled / Supervisors: approx 35%-40% higher than unskilled.

## Key Compliance Requirements in Karnataka
1. Karnataka Shops and Commercial Establishments Act, 1961 requires overtime pay at double the ordinary wage rate (2x).
2. Resignation / Separation: Under Payment of Wages Act Section 5(2), final settlement must be paid before the expiry of the 2nd working day.
3. Portal: e-Karmika (Karnataka Labour Dept online service) and Karmika Bhavan, Bannerghatta Road, Bengaluru 560029.
