# Statutory Minimum Wages — Government of NCT of Delhi
Notification Reference: Order F.No.12(142)/02/MW/VII/Partfile/5623. Effective from 01 April 2024.

## Statutory Minimum Wage Rates by Skill Category (Effective 01-04-2024)
In exercise of powers conferred under Section 5(2) of the Minimum Wages Act, 1948, the Government of NCT of Delhi revises the dearness allowance and statutory minimum rates of wages payable to all categories of workers employed in all scheduled employments in the National Capital Territory of Delhi. Payment of any wage below these statutory rates is an offence under Section 22 of the Minimum Wages Act, 1948.

- **Unskilled Workers**:
  - Daily Rate: ₹695.00 per day
  - Monthly Rate: ₹18,066.00 per month
  - Sectors Covered: Construction (Building Operations & Road Construction), Retail & Commercial (Shops & Establishments), Manufacturing & Factories (Engineering & Metal Works), Security & Facility (Watch and Ward), Hospitality & Food Services (Hotels & Restaurants), Domestic Work (Cleaners & Helpers), Agriculture & Allied Operations, Transport & Logistics (Loading and Unloading Goods).

- **Semi-Skilled Workers**:
  - Daily Rate: ₹766.00 per day
  - Monthly Rate: ₹19,929.00 per month
  - Typical roles: Assistant artisans, machine helpers, junior cooks, delivery drivers with light vehicle license.

- **Skilled Workers**:
  - Daily Rate: ₹844.00 per day
  - Monthly Rate: ₹21,917.00 per month
  - Typical roles: Electricians, plumbers, masons, machinists, senior cooks, heavy vehicle drivers, supervisors.

- **Clerical and Supervisory Staff**:
  - Non-Matriculates: ₹766.00 daily / ₹19,929.00 monthly
  - Matriculates but not Graduates: ₹844.00 daily / ₹21,917.00 monthly
  - Graduates and above: ₹918.00 daily / ₹23,836.00 monthly

## Enforcement, Working Hours, and Overtime Rules
1. Working Hours & Weekly Off: The normal working day is 8 hours (exclusive of meal interval). Every worker is entitled to one paid day of rest in every period of 7 days (Rule 23 of Delhi Minimum Wages Rules, 1950).
2. Overtime Rate: Overtime work beyond 8 hours a day or 48 hours a week must be paid at double (2x) the ordinary rate of wages under Section 14 of the Minimum Wages Act.
3. Claims Authority: Labour Welfare Centre, Pusa Road / District Labour Commissioner Offices across Delhi.
