# Statutory Minimum Wages — State of Tamil Nadu
Notification Reference: G.O.(2D) No. 44 Labour Welfare and Skill Development Department. Effective from 01 April 2024.

## Overview and Zonal Structure
Tamil Nadu prescribes minimum wages across more than 90 scheduled employments. Zonal divisions classify Zone A (Corporations of Chennai, Coimbatore, Madurai, Tiruchirappalli, Salem, Tirunelveli, Tiruppur, Erode, Vellore, Thoothukudi), Zone B (Municipalities), and Zone C (Town Panchayats and Village Panchayats). Dearness Allowance is adjusted annually based on the Consumer Price Index for Chennai City with base year 2001=100. Effective 01 April 2024, the VDA is ₹105.00 per day.

## Scheduled Rates by Sector in Zone A (Unskilled Category)
- **Construction (Building and Construction Industry)**:
  - Basic: ₹410.00/day + VDA: ₹105.00/day = Total Daily: ₹515.00
  - Total Monthly: ₹13,390.00 per month
- **Retail & Commercial (Shops and Commercial Establishments)**:
  - Basic: ₹400.00/day + VDA: ₹105.00/day = Total Daily: ₹505.00
  - Total Monthly: ₹13,130.00 per month
- **Manufacturing & Factories (Textiles, Auto Components, General Engineering)**:
  - Basic: ₹395.00/day + VDA: ₹105.00/day = Total Daily: ₹500.00
  - Total Monthly: ₹13,000.00 per month
- **Security & Facility (Security Services and Watch & Ward)**:
  - Basic: ₹430.00/day + VDA: ₹105.00/day = Total Daily: ₹535.00
  - Total Monthly: ₹13,910.00 per month
- **Hospitality & Food Services (Hotels and Restaurants)**:
  - Basic: ₹385.00/day + VDA: ₹105.00/day = Total Daily: ₹490.00
  - Total Monthly: ₹12,740.00 per month
- **Domestic Work (Domestic Workers and Cleaners)**:
  - Basic: ₹370.00/day + VDA: ₹105.00/day = Total Daily: ₹475.00
  - Total Monthly: ₹12,350.00 per month
- **Agriculture & Allied (Agricultural Labour and Plantations)**:
  - Basic: ₹340.00/day + VDA: ₹105.00/day = Total Daily: ₹445.00
  - Total Monthly: ₹11,570.00 per month
- **Transport & Logistics (Loading, Unloading Goods and Road Transport)**:
  - Basic: ₹405.00/day + VDA: ₹105.00/day = Total Daily: ₹510.00
  - Total Monthly: ₹13,260.00 per month

## Statutory Deductions & Overtime Rules
1. Tamil Nadu Shops and Establishments Act, 1947 & Minimum Wages Rules: Overtime is strictly payable at twice the ordinary rate of wages (2x).
2. Deductions cannot exceed 50% under Section 7(3) of the Payment of Wages Act.
3. Authority: Office of the Commissioner of Labour, DMS Complex, Teynampet, Chennai 600006.
