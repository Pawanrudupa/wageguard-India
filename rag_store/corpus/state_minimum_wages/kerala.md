# Statutory Minimum Wages — State of Kerala
Notification Reference: S.R.O. No. 112/2024 Labour and Skills Department. Effective 01 January 2024.

## Overview and High Statutory Floor
Kerala maintains some of the highest statutory minimum wage rates in the country across over 80 scheduled employments. Cost of Living Allowance (Consumer Price Index linked dearness allowance) is revised periodically. For the period beginning January 2024, the applicable VDA is ₹90.00 per day.

## Scheduled Rates by Sector (Unskilled Category, Statewide Rates)
- **Construction (Building and Road Construction)**:
  - Basic: ₹480.00/day + VDA: ₹90.00/day = Total Daily: ₹570.00
  - Total Monthly: ₹14,820.00 per month
- **Retail & Commercial (Shops and Commercial Establishments)**:
  - Basic: ₹450.00/day + VDA: ₹90.00/day = Total Daily: ₹540.00
  - Total Monthly: ₹14,040.00 per month
- **Manufacturing & Factories (Coir, Cashew, Light Engineering, Processing)**:
  - Basic: ₹460.00/day + VDA: ₹90.00/day = Total Daily: ₹550.00
  - Total Monthly: ₹14,300.00 per month
- **Security & Facility (Security Guard Personnel and Watch and Ward)**:
  - Basic: ₹500.00/day + VDA: ₹90.00/day = Total Daily: ₹590.00
  - Total Monthly: ₹15,340.00 per month
- **Hospitality & Food Services (Hotels and Restaurants)**:
  - Basic: ₹440.00/day + VDA: ₹90.00/day = Total Daily: ₹530.00
  - Total Monthly: ₹13,780.00 per month
- **Domestic Work (Domestic Workers and Cleaners)**:
  - Basic: ₹430.00/day + VDA: ₹90.00/day = Total Daily: ₹520.00
  - Total Monthly: ₹13,520.00 per month
- **Agriculture & Allied (Plantations - Rubber, Tea, Coffee, Cardamom)**:
  - Basic: ₹420.00/day + VDA: ₹90.00/day = Total Daily: ₹510.00
  - Total Monthly: ₹13,260.00 per month
- **Transport & Logistics (Headload and Transport Workers)**:
  - Basic: ₹490.00/day + VDA: ₹90.00/day = Total Daily: ₹580.00
  - Total Monthly: ₹15,080.00 per month (Covered by Kerala Headload Workers Act, 1978)

## Overtime and Worker Welfare Protections
1. Headload Workers Act and Kerala Shops and Commercial Establishments Act ensure statutory social security and welfare board coverage.
2. Overtime is payable at 2x the normal hourly wage.
3. Resignation / Termination dues must be settled within 2 working days per Section 5(2) of Payment of Wages Act.
4. Authority: Labour Commissionerate, Thozhil Bhavan, Vikas Bhavan P.O., Thiruvananthapuram 695033.
