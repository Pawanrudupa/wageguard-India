# Statutory Minimum Wages — State of West Bengal
Notification Reference: Order No. 12/Stat/2RW/29/16/LCS/JLC. Effective 01 January 2024 to 30 June 2024 (Bi-annually revisable in January and July).

## Overview and Zonal Classifications
West Bengal prescribes minimum wage rates for 88 scheduled employments. Zone A covers areas under Kolkata Municipal Corporation, Howrah, Bidhannagar, Siliguri, Asansol, and other municipal corporation areas and industrial belts. Zone B covers other non-municipal rural areas. For the period beginning January 2024, the applicable Consumer Price Index linked VDA in Zone A is ₹63.00 per day.

## Scheduled Rates by Sector in Zone A (Kolkata & Industrial Urban Areas, Unskilled Category)
- **Construction (Construction and Maintenance of Roads and Buildings)**:
  - Basic: ₹355.00/day + VDA: ₹63.00/day = Total Daily: ₹418.00
  - Total Monthly: ₹10,868.00 per month
- **Retail & Commercial (Shops and Commercial Establishments)**:
  - Basic: ₹350.00/day + VDA: ₹63.00/day = Total Daily: ₹413.00
  - Total Monthly: ₹10,738.00 per month
- **Manufacturing & Factories (General Engineering, Jute, Foundry)**:
  - Basic: ₹352.00/day + VDA: ₹63.00/day = Total Daily: ₹415.00
  - Total Monthly: ₹10,790.00 per month
- **Security & Facility (Private Security Services and Watchmen)**:
  - Basic: ₹365.00/day + VDA: ₹63.00/day = Total Daily: ₹428.00
  - Total Monthly: ₹11,128.00 per month
- **Hospitality & Food Services (Hotels, Restaurants, Sweets and Eating Houses)**:
  - Basic: ₹345.00/day + VDA: ₹63.00/day = Total Daily: ₹408.00
  - Total Monthly: ₹10,608.00 per month
- **Domestic Work (Domestic Workers and Cleaners)**:

- **Agriculture & Allied (Agricultural Operations and Tea Plantations)**:

- **Transport & Logistics (Public Motor Transport and Cargo Handling)**:
  - Basic: ₹355.00/day + VDA: ₹63.00/day = Total Daily: ₹418.00
  - Total Monthly: ₹10,868.00 per month

## Overtime and Working Hours Rules
1. West Bengal Minimum Wages Rules, 1951: Normal working hours are 8 hours per day, 48 hours per week.
2. Overtime Rate: Overtime must be paid at double (2x) the ordinary rate of wages.
3. Resignation / Separation: Under Payment of Wages Act Section 5(2), wages must be paid before the expiry of the 2nd working day following separation.
4. Authority: Office of the Labour Commissioner, New Secretariat Buildings, 11th Floor, 1, K.S. Roy Road, Kolkata 700001.
