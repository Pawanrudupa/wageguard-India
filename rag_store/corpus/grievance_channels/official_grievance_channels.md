# Official Labour Grievance Filing Channels & Dispute Resolution

## Central Portals and Universal Channels

### 1. Shram Suvidha Portal (Ministry of Labour & Employment)
- **Portal URL**: https://shramsuvidha.gov.in
- **Scope**: Central labour law compliance, inspection complaints, reporting violations of Minimum Wages Act and Payment of Wages Act in Central sphere establishments (railways, major ports, mines, oilfields, banking/insurance, central PSUs).
- **Process**: Register using mobile number/Aadhaar, select 'File Grievance/Violation', choose establishment, specify violation category (e.g. non-payment of minimum wages, illegal deductions), and track inspection status online.
- **Toll-Free Helpline**: 1800-180-1555 (Central Labour Commissioner Helpdesk).

### 2. EPFO EPFiGMS (Employees' Provident Fund Internet Grievance Management System)
- **Portal URL**: https://epfigms.gov.in
- **Scope**: Employer non-deposit of PF contributions, deduction of PF from salary without depositing into EPFO account, delayed PF settlement, transfer issues, pension scheme (EPS) grievances.
- **Key Legal Mandate**: Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952. Deducting PF from an employee's salary and failing to deposit it with EPFO within 15 days of the following month is a criminal breach of trust under Section 405/406 of the Indian Penal Code (IPC) and an offence under Section 14 of the EPF Act.
- **Process**: Worker enters UAN (Universal Account Number) or PPO number, uploads salary slip showing PF deduction, and logs the complaint. EPFO mandates investigation within 30 days.
- **EPFO Toll-Free Helpline**: 1800-118-005.

### 3. ESIC Grievance Portal & CPGRAMS (Employees' State Insurance)
- **Portal URL**: https://www.esic.gov.in / https://pgportal.gov.in
- **Scope**: Medical benefits denial, employer non-registration under ESI, failure to deposit ESI contributions (0.75% employee + 3.25% employer contribution for employees earning up to ₹21,000/month).
- **ESIC Toll-Free Helpline**: 1800-112-526 / 1800-113-839.

### 4. NALSA Free Legal Services (National Legal Services Authority)
- **National Toll-Free Legal Aid Helpline**: **15100** (Available 24x7 across India).
- **Portal URL**: https://nalsa.gov.in
- **Legal Entitlement**: Under Section 12(h) of the Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987, industrial workmen, low-income citizens, women, and unorganised sector workers are statutorily entitled to **completely free legal aid**, including free court-appointed advocates to file wage-recovery suits before Labour Courts and Payment of Wages Authorities.

## State-Specific Labour Commissioner Contact & Portals

### Delhi
- **Department**: Labour Department, Government of NCT of Delhi
- **Portal**: https://labour.delhi.gov.in / e-District Delhi: https://edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in
- **Grievance Helpline**: 011-23813845 / 1031 (Delhi Govt Citizen Helpline)
- **Head Office**: 5-Sham Nath Marg, Civil Lines, Delhi - 110054
- **Procedure**: File a formal claim under Section 15 of Payment of Wages Act or Section 20 of Minimum Wages Act at the District Labour Office (e.g. Pusa Road for West District, Pushpa Bhawan for South).

### Maharashtra
- **Department**: Office of the Labour Commissioner, Government of Maharashtra
- **Portal**: https://mahakamgar.maharashtra.gov.in
- **Toll-Free Helpline**: 1800-889-2816 / 022-26572631
- **Head Office**: Kamgar Bhavan, 'E' Block, C-20, Bandra-Kurla Complex, Bandra (East), Mumbai - 400051
- **Procedure**: Aggrieved workers can register complaints via the MahaKamgar grievance module or visit the local Assistant Labour Commissioner (ALC) office.

### Karnataka
- **Department**: Department of Labour, Government of Karnataka
- **Portal**: https://labour.karnataka.gov.in / e-Karmika: https://ekarmika.karnataka.gov.in
- **Toll-Free Helpline**: 155214 / 080-22266180
- **Head Office**: Karmika Bhavan, Dairy Circle, Bannerghatta Road, Bengaluru - 560029
- **Procedure**: Submit online grievance on e-Karmika or approach the jurisdictional Labour Officer / Industrial Tribunal under the Karnataka Shops & Establishments Act or Minimum Wages Act.

### Tamil Nadu
- **Department**: Labour Welfare and Skill Development Department, Government of Tamil Nadu
- **Portal**: https://labour.tn.gov.in
- **Helpline**: 044-24335147 / 044-24335149
- **Head Office**: DMS Complex, Teynampet, Chennai - 600006
- **Procedure**: Complaints regarding wage denial, illegal termination, or overtime can be filed with the Inspector of Labour or Deputy Commissioner of Labour in the respective district.

### Kerala
- **Department**: Labour and Skills Department, Government of Kerala
- **Portal**: https://lc.kerala.gov.in
- **Labour Helpline**: 1800-425-55214 / 0471-2301249
- **Head Office**: Labour Commissionerate, Thozhil Bhavan, Vikas Bhavan P.O., Thiruvananthapuram - 695033
- **Procedure**: Lodge complaints through the Shramik online portal or contact the District Labour Officer (DLO). Headload workers can also approach the Kerala Headload Workers Welfare Board.

### Telangana
- **Department**: Department of Labour, Employment, Training & Factories, Government of Telangana
- **Portal**: https://labour.telangana.gov.in
- **Helpline**: 040-27633215 / 040-27633216
- **Head Office**: T. Anjaiah Bhavan, RTC Cross Roads, Musheerabad, Hyderabad - 500020
- **Procedure**: Complaints regarding unpaid salaries, forced deductions, or shops & establishments violations are processed through the Joint Commissioner of Labour.

### West Bengal
- **Department**: Labour Department, Government of West Bengal
- **Portal**: https://wblabour.gov.in
- **Toll-Free Helpline**: 1800-103-0009
- **Head Office**: New Secretariat Buildings, 11th Floor, 1, K.S. Roy Road, Kolkata - 700001
- **Procedure**: Workers can submit complaints via the 'Samadhan' online grievance tracking portal or approach the Regional Labour Office (RLO) in their municipal borough or district.
